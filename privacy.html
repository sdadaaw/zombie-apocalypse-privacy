This information may include device identifiers such as the advertising ID, IP address, approximate location inferred from the IP address, app interactions, ad views, ad clicks, diagnostic information, crash logs, performance data, and other technical information about your device and app usage.

We do not directly collect or store this advertising information on our own servers. The information is processed by Google according to Google's policies.

We use advertising services to show ads in the game, reward players who choose to watch rewarded video ads, support app operation, prevent abuse or invalid traffic, measure ad performance, and improve game stability.

Ads shown in the game may be personalized or non-personalized depending on your device settings, region, consent choices, and Google's advertising policies.

Rewarded video ads are optional. If you choose not to watch a rewarded video ad, you may not receive the related in-game reward, but you can continue using the game.
